## What Is It?

The recurring "why did you choose X over Y?" decisions in AI systems, and how to
reason about them out loud.

## Why Does It Matter?

There's rarely one right answer. Interviewers want to see you weigh options
against requirements (latency, cost, quality, privacy).

## Core Tradeoffs

### RAG vs Fine-tuning

| | RAG | Fine-tuning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fresh/changing data | Easy (update the store) | Retrain to update |
| Grounding/citations | Yes | Not inherently |
| Upfront cost | Lower | Higher (training) |
| Teaches style/format | Weak | Strong |

Rule of thumb: **RAG for knowledge**, **fine-tuning for behavior/format**. They
compose.

### Latency vs Quality

More retrieval, reranking, and bigger models improve answers but add latency.
Budget per stage; cache; use a reranker only when it earns its cost.

### Cost vs Quality

LLM tokens dominate cost. Levers: smaller/local models, lower top-k, caching,
shorter prompts, and truncating context to what's needed.

### Consistency vs Availability

Right after ingest, is stale retrieval acceptable for a moment? Usually yes —
favor availability and eventual consistency for the knowledge base.

### Chunk Size / Top-k

Small chunks + higher top-k = precise but fragmented; large chunks + low top-k =
more context but more noise. Tune with [evaluation](../06-advanced-rag/03-evaluation.mdx).

### Local vs Hosted Models

Local (Ollama): privacy, no per-token cost, control. Hosted: top-tier quality,
no infra. Choose on data sensitivity, budget, and quality needs.

## How to Answer Tradeoff Questions

1. Restate the requirement that matters (latency? cost? freshness?).
2. Give the options and their pros/cons.
3. Make a recommendation **for this context**.
4. Note how you'd validate it (metrics/eval).

## Common Mistakes

- Absolute claims ("X is always better").
- Ignoring the stated requirements.
- No plan to measure the decision.

## Interview Questions

- RAG vs fine-tuning — when each?
- How do you reduce latency without wrecking quality?
- How do you control LLM cost?
- Local vs hosted models — how do you decide?

## Things to Remember

- Tie every decision to a requirement.
- RAG = knowledge; fine-tuning = behavior.
- Always say how you'd measure the choice.
