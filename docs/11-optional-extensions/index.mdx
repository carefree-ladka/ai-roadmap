## Goal

Extend your foundation into specialized or advanced areas as your interests and
job needs dictate.

## Estimated Time

Ongoing.

## Prerequisites

- The full core roadmap ([01 · Python](../01-python/index.mdx) through [10 · System Design & Interview](../10-system-design-interview/index.mdx)).

## Directions to Explore

### Multimodal RAG

Retrieve over images, tables, and PDFs — not just text. Use vision models and
layout-aware parsing; embed images and captions.

### Advanced Agents

Multi-tool, multi-step agents with planning, tool-use guardrails, and
human-in-the-loop. Revisit the cautions in [Memory & Agents](../07-langchain-lcel/09-memory-agents.mdx).

### Fine-tuning & Adapters

LoRA/QLoRA to teach style, format, or a narrow domain. Recall: fine-tuning for
**behavior**, RAG for **knowledge** (see [tradeoffs](../10-system-design-interview/02-tradeoff-discussions.mdx)).

### GraphRAG / Structured Retrieval

Combine knowledge graphs or SQL with vector search for multi-hop questions and
precise structured lookups.

### Query Transformations

HyDE (hypothetical document embeddings), multi-query expansion, and step-back
prompting to improve retrieval recall.

### Streaming UIs

Stream tokens to a frontend (Server-Sent Events / WebSockets) for a responsive
chat experience over your `/ask` endpoint.

### Advanced Evaluation & Observability

Automated eval pipelines in CI, LLM tracing dashboards, and regression tests on
answer quality.

### Cost & Performance Optimization

Quantized models, batching, semantic caching, and speculative decoding.

## How to Approach These

Pick **one** extension, apply it to your existing RAG project, and measure the
impact with your [eval set](../06-advanced-rag/03-evaluation.mdx). Depth on one
beats shallow exposure to all.

## Interview Relevance

Knowing these exist — and their tradeoffs — signals you can grow a system beyond
the basics, even if you haven't shipped all of them.

## Done When

- [ ] You've chosen an extension aligned with your goals.
- [ ] You've applied it to your project and measured the effect.
- [ ] You can explain its tradeoffs.
